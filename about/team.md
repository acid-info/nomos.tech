Nomos is being built by a team of passionate and experienced blockchain developers and researchers. Alongside Nomos' project lead is a team of researchers and developers with experience as software engineers both within and beyond the blockchain and cypherpunk ecosystem, as well as in academia.

Nomos is keen to work with other contributors. If you share our passion for decentralisation, individual sovereignty and privacy, we'd love to hear from you. As an open-source project, we also welcome community contributions. Please check out our [Github repo here](https://github.com/logos-co).
